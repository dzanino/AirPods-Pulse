1. What data the app processes

  • Microphone audio (iPhone or AirPods) – processed in real time on the device for filtering, amplification and indicative metrics. Nothing is stored until you tap Record.
  • Recordings (WAV) and metadata (time, auscultation site, notes, indicative results) – only in the app folder on the device; backed up with your iPhone according to your iCloud Backup settings.
  • Settings (probe mode, filter, gain, disclaimer acceptance) – stored locally on the device.

2. What the app does not do

  • It does not send audio, recordings or results to any server.
  • It collects no analytics, device identifiers or location.
  • It contains no ads or third-party SDKs and requires no account.

3. Health (HealthKit) and Apple Watch

The app reads only heart rate and the latest ECG classification from Apple Watch to compare the wrist pulse with beats heard over the heart (pulse deficit). It writes nothing to Health; the short watch measurement is discarded when it ends and is not saved as a workout. Health data never leaves the device, is never used for marketing or advertising and is never shared with third parties – in line with Apple’s HealthKit rules.

4. Sharing

If you use the Share button, the iOS share sheet hands the chosen file to the app you pick (Mail, Messages, AirDrop…). From then on that service’s terms apply. Do not share other people’s recordings without their consent.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Because data never leaves your device, the controller has no access to it and does not process it. Deletion: delete recordings in the app or uninstall the app. Questions: jansikuta@me.com.
